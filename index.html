Meet Mofusand

Mofusand is a massively popular Japanese character series by Juno, featuring tiny, adorable fluffy cats in whimsical costumes like Shark Cat, Strawberry Cat, and Rabbit Cat. What started as cute illustrations on social media has become a huge cultural hit with anime and merchandise.
